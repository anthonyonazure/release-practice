Release Practice

A calm, free tool for the moment a hard feeling shows up: notice it, allow it, and let it go.

What it will be

  • Six first-aid buttons for common moments: Too Much, Stuck, Can't Feel, Came Back, Not Working, Want It Gone.
  • A 90-second Allow, a short guided timer to sit with a feeling without fighting it.
  • An Evening Clean-Up to close the day.
  • A monthly progress view so you can see what has been shifting.
Private by design. Your entries stay on your own device. No account, no tracking of what you write, no AI in the practice itself.

Who it is for

Readers of books on the surrender and letting-go tradition who want a simple daily companion to the practice. It is a self-reflection aid, not medical or mental-health care, and it does not replace your doctor or your treatment. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 in the US.

Release Practice is an independent project. Working title; the final name will be announced here.
